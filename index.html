Open schemas

JSON Schemas (draft 2020-12) for the files Atrekos writes and reads, generated from the product's own type definitions.

The schemas describe shape only. They carry no framework text and confer no conformity: a document that validates against a schema is well formed, not compliant with anything.

A frozen description for readers of older archives; it is never produced any more.

pack-artifacthttps://data.atrekos.com/schemas/pack-artifact/3.json 3 artifactVersion 3 A provisioning artifact: a pack, a model snapshot, five catalogue versions, and the frameworks the pack's revision assigns by label and content digest. Envelope versions 1 and 2 are refused by the loader and are not offered.
framework-filehttps://data.atrekos.com/schemas/framework-file/1.json 1 framework files (all versions) A framework library file: one descriptor and its controls. This schema is the shape level (VX-schema). The rules beyond shape stay in the application and run on upload: VX1 unique control ids, VX2 parents that exist and no cycles, VX3 declared levels, VX4 declared attribute values, VX5 relations that resolve, VX6 binding coverage, VX7 the text policy and its allowed processing.
atrekos-stix-extensionhttps://data.atrekos.com/schemas/atrekos-stix-extension/1.json 1 STIX 2.1 pack exports The property extension a STIX 2.1 pack export carries (#776 M4): the whole attack step or countermeasure on its attack-pattern or course-of-action, and on the identity that stands for the pack its questions, damage scenarios, assumptions, licence, provenance and the lossy-projection marker. The pack artifact, not the STIX bundle, is canonical.
